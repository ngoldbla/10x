## 2. Shot-by-shot inventory

One block per shot. This section is deliberately mechanical: it forces looking
before concluding, and it gives every later finding something to point at.

### `B{bb}-S01_{surface}-{scene}-{state}_{appearance}`

- **What is on screen.** Everything visible, top to bottom. Include the things
  that are easy not to see — safe-area gaps, hairlines, empty regions.
- **Element count.** The number of distinct visible elements (§5.1).
- **This screen is about:** one sentence, no "and" (§5.2).
- **Weight vs. importance.** Rank what matters; rank what is loud. Note the
  disagreements (§5.3).
- **Estimated bare-ground fraction.** Roughly what share of the area is
  untouched, against the app's own 0.28 ceiling (§1.1).

*(repeat per shot)*

## 4. Findings

Ordered **P0 first, then P1, then P2**. Use the shape from `CRITERIA.md` §7.

### F-B{bb}-01 · P{0|1|2} · shots `{id}`, `{id}`

**Claim.** One sentence — what is wrong, as a fact about the picture.

**Evidence.** What is literally visible. Counts, sizes, positions, relative
weights. No inference here.

**Why it matters.** Cite the law: charter rule (§1.x), numeric token (§2.x),
platform idiom (§3), ADA lens (§4), or parsimony test (§5.x). If it violates
nothing codified and is still wrong, say that explicitly — those are often the
most valuable findings, and the capstone may promote them into new law.

**Direction.** What to do instead. Prefer a *fork* ("decide whether this is a
board or a workstation, then…") to a patch ("increase the padding to 20").

*(repeat)*

## 5. Unverified suspicions

Things you believe are wrong but cannot prove from these frames — a motion you
can't see in a still, a state not captured, a device-only material effect.
**Not findings.** The capstone may use them; a design prompt may not cite one as
its sole justification.

- **{suspicion}** — what evidence would settle it.

## 6. Cross-references

- Findings here that are probably the *same defect* as one in another bundle
  (give both IDs). The synthesis stage collapses these, and it can only do that
  if you flag them.
- Findings here that *contradict* another bundle's — equally valuable, and they
  usually mean a platform is diverging without meaning to.
